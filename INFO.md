# Reap × 65labs Agentic Buildathon

**Singapore · Friday, 9 October 2026 · Hosted by Reap and 65labs**

## Brief

Build a product with an AI agent paying for something at its core. Reap provides a payments sandbox so agents can purchase within permissions and spending controls.

The event includes a Reap product and API walkthrough, building time with the team available to help, drinks, and dinner. Solo builders are welcome.

## Build tracks

| Track | Guiding question |
| --- | --- |
| Business spend | What becomes automatic when an agent can buy, pay, and reconcile within budget? |
| Everyday life | Which everyday spending should an agent handle for you or someone you care for? |
| Commerce in chat and apps | What if buying and getting paid happened inside the apps people already use? |
| Stablecoins and the agent economy | What can agents do when money is programmable? |

## Prize tracks

| Prize | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Most Worthwhile Problem | A product real users would download and pay for |
| Best Bridge Between Onchain and Real World | A link from stablecoins to real-world spending |
